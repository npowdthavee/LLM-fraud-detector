# 1. Data collected

- Scenario ratings: legitimacy, financial risk, scam/fraud red flags, advisability, and categorical classification.
- Broad finance-background categories: years of experience, area of expertise, and broad qualification/background categories.
- No names, email addresses, telephone numbers, employer names, precise job titles, mailing addresses, NRIC/passport numbers, Prolific IDs, or other direct identifiers will be requested in the Qualtrics survey.

# 2. Anonymity and platform separation

The Qualtrics instrument will be configured so that survey responses do not record IP address or location information and no Prolific ID is passed into Qualtrics. Prolific independently maintains account information necessary for recruitment and payment. The research team will not create or retain a linkage key connecting a Prolific account to a particular survey response.

# 3. Access and storage

- Raw survey exports will be downloaded from Qualtrics to NTU-approved, access-controlled institutional storage.
- Access will be limited to the PI and authorized study team members on a need-to-know basis.
- No participant-level files will be stored on personal cloud accounts or shared through unsecured channels.
- Any temporary local download used for analysis will be on an encrypted/password-protected NTU-managed or otherwise NTU-approved device and deleted after transfer to the approved storage location.

# 4. Data classification

The study is designed to generate anonymous research data. Nonetheless, all research files will be handled conservatively under NTU research-data and data-governance requirements. The small sample size is an additional reason not to release participant-level background combinations publicly.

# 5. Retention

Research records will be retained for 10 years after publication or after completion of the project, whichever is later, unless a longer period is required by NTU policy, law, or an investigation/audit.

# 6. Analysis files

Analysis files will contain scenario codes, rating variables, and broad background categories only. Derived files will include medians, category agreement counts, and pass/fail determinations against the prespecified validation criteria.

# 7. Sharing and dissemination

Individual-level expert-validation data will not be posted publicly as part of the later paper. Aggregate validation outcomes may be described in methods documentation. Any future sharing beyond the approved scope will follow applicable NTU policies and IRB requirements.

# 8. Data destruction

At the end of the required retention period, research records will be securely destroyed in accordance with NTU policy and any applicable legal or audit requirements.
