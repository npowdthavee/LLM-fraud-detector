# 1. Basis for Exempt Category 2

This study is an anonymous online survey of adults. It involves no intervention, manipulation of the participant’s environment, deception, biological samples, medical procedures, or collection/linkage of individually identifiable research data. Participants evaluate fictional investment scenarios and answer a small number of broad finance-background questions.

The study is designed to satisfy the key characteristics of NTU Exempt Category 2: survey procedures; adult participants; information recorded without direct or linked identifiers; and no invasive or sensitive questions likely to cause emotional distress or reputational, legal, financial, or employability harm if disclosed.

# 2. Minimal-risk assessment

The expected risk is no greater than ordinary professional judgment tasks. Participants are not asked about their own finances, experiences of fraud, employer, job performance, health, politics, illegal activity, or other sensitive matters. The scenarios are hypothetical. The principal foreseeable burden is mild fatigue from the 20–25 minute survey.

# 3. Electronic consent and waiver of signed documentation

The study will obtain informed consent electronically before any study procedures. The research team requests that signed or typed-name documentation of consent not be required because the study is anonymous and collecting a signature/name would create the only direct link between the participant and the research. Participants will instead indicate consent by selecting an affirmative consent option in Qualtrics after reading the Study Information Sheet. Those who do not consent will not proceed to study questions.

This is a request to waive written documentation/signature only; it is not a request to waive the informed-consent process.

# 4. Privacy protections supporting exemption

- Age eligibility and finance background are handled through Prolific prescreening; the study survey confirms consent and records only broad, non-identifying background categories.
- Qualtrics is configured not to record IP address/location and no Prolific ID is embedded in the survey URL or dataset.
- No linkage file between Prolific accounts and Qualtrics responses will be created.
- Only aggregate validation summaries are expected to be described in later research documentation.

# 5. Why the survey is necessary

Independent expert validation is needed to reduce researcher subjectivity in classifying the hypothetical investment scenarios before they are frozen in a later preregistered AI experiment. The validation is therefore a limited preparatory human-subjects component, separate from the subsequent model-only confirmatory data collection.
