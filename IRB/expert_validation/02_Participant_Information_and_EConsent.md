# Invitation

You are invited to take part in a research study conducted by Nanyang Technological University (NTU). Please read the information below before deciding whether to participate.

# Why is this study being conducted?

We are developing hypothetical investment scenarios for use in research on AI-generated financial advice. Before using the scenarios, we would like people with a finance-related background to evaluate how legitimate, risky, and advisable the opportunities appear, and whether they contain signs associated with scams or fraud.

# Why have I been invited?

You have been invited because you are at least 21 years old and matched finance-related recruitment criteria on Prolific. The survey is in English.

# What will I do?

You will read 14 hypothetical investment opportunities, shown in a random order. For each, you will provide four ratings from 0 to 100 and select the category that best describes the opportunity. You will also answer a few broad questions about your finance-related background. The survey should take about 20–25 minutes.

# Are there any risks or discomforts?

The study is expected to involve no more than minimal risk. The scenarios are fictional. We will not ask about your own investments, financial losses, scams, employer, health, or other sensitive personal information. You may find the survey mildly tiring because it asks you to evaluate several scenarios.

# Are there any benefits?

There is no direct personal benefit. Your responses may help improve the quality of research materials used in a later study of AI financial advice.

# Will I be paid?

Yes. If you complete the study, you will receive £6.25 for an estimated 25-minute survey (equivalent to approximately £15/hour), paid through Prolific in accordance with the platform’s procedures.

# Do I have to participate?

No. Participation is voluntary. You may decline to participate or stop the survey at any time before submitting your response, without penalty. If you do not consent, you will be redirected using the Prolific no-consent completion path.

# How will my privacy be protected?

The research survey is designed to be anonymous. The Qualtrics survey will not ask for your name, email address, telephone number, employer name, or Prolific ID, and it will be configured not to record your IP address or location data. Prolific will handle recruitment and payment separately. The research team will not create a file linking your Prolific account to your survey answers.

Because your survey response is anonymous, after you submit it we will generally not be able to identify which response is yours in order to delete it.

# How will the data be used and stored?

The responses will be used to decide whether the hypothetical investment scenarios are suitable for a later research study. Individual responses are not intended to be reported as substantive outcomes in that later paper. Data will be stored on access-controlled NTU-approved institutional storage and retained in accordance with NTU research-data requirements. Results will only be reported in a form that does not identify individual participants.
